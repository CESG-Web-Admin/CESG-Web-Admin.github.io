## Details
The 42nd Annual Meeting of the **Canadian Econometrics Study Group (CESG)** will be held in Montreal (Quebec) in 2027. The meeting is jointly organized by McGill University and Université du Québec à Montréal (UQAM). Further details, including the dates, venue and keynote speakers, will be announced soon.
